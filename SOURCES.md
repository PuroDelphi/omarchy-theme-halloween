# Wallpaper provenance and licenses

**10 downloaded images + 2 original vector illustrations + 2 additional AI-generated images.** Files 01–10 are redistributed under the licenses stated by their sources; they are not presented as this project's creations. Files 11/12 are original vector illustrations; 13/14 are fictional horror scenes generated specifically with FLUX.1 [schnell].

Downloaded photographs are included **unmodified, at their source resolution and without artificial upscaling**. Desktop scaling/cropping does not change the distributed file. Detailed metadata, including download links and license notices, is retained in [`sources/01-05.json`](sources/01-05.json) and [`sources/06-10.json`](sources/06-10.json).

## Optional animated wallpaper

[`optional/halloween-jumpscare.webm`](optional/halloween-jumpscare.webm) is a silent **1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 24-second VP9 WebM** derived solely from the project's AI-generated images **13 and 14**. Changes: downscaling, a cut, a slow centered zoom, a two-second alpha fade and video encoding. The original WebP files are unchanged.

The same qualified dedication applies: the project's contribution is offered under **CC0 only to the extent applicable rights exist**, without a promise of exclusivity or copyright protection. No third-party photograph, music, font file or model weight is included in the video.

[`sources/optional-jumpscare.json`](sources/optional-jumpscare.json) records source SHA-256 hashes, the timeline, encoder version, exact FFmpeg command and final file hash. The video is not a desktop screenshot. It is excluded from the default background directory and requires explicit installation and selection on an Omarchy version with native video wallpaper support; see the [enable/disable instructions](README.md#optional-animated-edition).
