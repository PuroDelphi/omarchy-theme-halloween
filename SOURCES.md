# Wallpaper provenance and licenses

**14 still wallpapers = 10 licensed third-party downloads + 4 project-created works.** The four project-created wallpapers are **2 procedural vector illustrations (11–12)** and **2 FLUX-generated horror scenes (13–14)**. Files 01–10 retain their source licenses and are not presented as this project's creations. Preview images and the optional video are derivatives or presentations of these existing works, not additional independently created still wallpapers.

Downloaded photographs are included **unmodified, at their source resolution and without artificial upscaling**. Desktop scaling/cropping does not change the distributed file. Detailed metadata, including download links and license notices, is retained in [`sources/01-05.json`](sources/01-05.json) and [`sources/06-10.json`](sources/06-10.json).

## Optional animated wallpaper

[`optional/halloween-jumpscare.webm`](optional/halloween-jumpscare.webm) is a silent **736 × 400, 24 fps, 15.042-second VP9 WebM**, exported from an animation supplied by the repository owner for **13 — The Last Corridor**. It contains character movement and a final close-up scare with reaching hands and broken-glass imagery. Scene 14 is not used. The source has 361 frames; the export preserves their dimensions and timing without upscaling, cropping, interpolation or added transitions. The original MP4, including its audio, remains unchanged locally and is not included in the published asset set. The export omits audio, attached cover art and source container metadata. The fourteen still wallpapers are unchanged.

**Created with Grok.** The repository owner confirmed that Grok generated the animation and authorized its publication. The exact model version, prompt and generation settings were not supplied and are not inferred. These public references were checked on **2026-10-19**. Retain the **Created with Grok** credit with the distributed animation. The attribution does not imply provider endorsement. No blanket MIT or CC0 license is asserted for the supplied animation itself; the qualified CC0 dedication of the underlying still and only the project's own encoding contribution does not waive third-party rights or provider conditions.

[`sources/optional-jumpscare.json`](sources/optional-jumpscare.json) records the source MP4 and still-image SHA-256 hashes, native dimensions, frame count, encoder version, exact export command, final hash and publication status. This is not a desktop screenshot or a seamless loop: the final scare resets visibly to the opening. It is excluded from the default background directory and requires explicit installation and selection on an Omarchy version with native video wallpaper support; see the [enable/disable instructions](README.md#optional-animated-edition).
